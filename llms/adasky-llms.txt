# ADASKY

> ADASKY (Adasky Ltd.) is an Israeli automotive sensor company founded in January 2016 in Yokneam, Israel. It designs and manufactures automotive-grade long-wave infrared (LWIR) thermal imaging cameras and the perception software that runs on them, for ADAS, autonomous vehicles, and intelligent transportation infrastructure.

## Products

- [Automotive Thermal Camera (Viper)](https://www.adasky.com/adasky-thermal-camera/): Compact solid-state shutterless LWIR thermal camera for vehicle perception. ~2.6cm diameter x 4.3cm length, 50mK temperature sensitivity, object detection to ~300m and living-being classification beyond 200m. Onboard computer vision covers object detection and classification, free-space detection, and time-to-collision for forward collision warning and automatic emergency braking.
- [SharpVision ITS / V2X Camera](https://www.adasky.com/sharp-vision/): Thermal camera for intelligent transportation systems, connected corridors and smart-city infrastructure; detection of pedestrians, vehicles and obstacles beyond 200m in all weather.
